# Grammar Studio — University Grammar Library

## Topics

1. Parts of Speech, The Noun and The Article: 9 modules, 58 theory cards, 72 practice questions, 40 glossary terms. Articles are organised by noun class, proper-name type, special uses and modifiers.
2. The Pronoun and The Numeral: four 90-minute sessions, 32 practice questions, 20 glossary terms and a 60-mark take-home assessment.
3. The Adjective and The Adverb: syllabus outline only, marked In development.

## Sources

Topic 1 adapts grammar content from the four supplied presentations: Topic_1_Consolidation_Grammar_Lab_revised; Noun and Article 2; Grammar Noun. Article; Grammar_Term1_Group150. Theory cards identify relevant slide references. Repetitive slide exercises are represented by selected contextual adaptations. Administrative, contact and semester-two preview sections are excluded. Overgeneralised source rules are corrected with contextual contrasts.

Kaushanskaya is the exam terminology reference. Supplementary references: How English Works, Practical English Usage, Downing, A Student’s Introduction to English Grammar. TKT informs teaching and assessment. The Good Grammar Book is excluded.
