# Topic 2 — actual coverage for checking against Kaushanskaya

This is a coverage map of the implemented course, not a claim to reproduce every paragraph or example in the textbook. Kaushanskaya’s traditional categories remain the examination reference; modern usage notes are labelled.

## Session 1: pronoun system and reference
- Pronoun definition; classification versus sentence function.
- Referent versus antecedent; textual and situational reference; ambiguity.
- Personal pronouns: person, number, case and third-person gender; nominative/objective forms, coordination and prepositions; singular they.
- Referential and dummy it: weather, time and anticipatory it.
- Possessive forms: conjoint/dependent versus absolute/independent; association, double possessives, its/it’s, identical his forms.
- Reflexive versus emphatic/intensive uses; by oneself; ordinary wash/dress; avoiding myself as a formal me.
- Reciprocal pronouns: mutual action, possessive forms; Kaushanskaya’s traditional each other/one another tendency contrasted with flexible contemporary usage.
- Demonstratives: this/that/these/those, temporal/spatial/discourse reference, such and (the) same.

## Session 2: indefinite, negative and defining groups
- Some/any and compounds; offers, requests, questions, negatives and free-choice any.
- No/none/nobody/no one/nothing/neither; negative constructions; agreement and singular they.
- All/both/each/every and every-compounds: totality and distribution, each/every and everyone/every one.
- Either/neither and of-patterns, two alternatives, agreement variants.
- Other/another/others/the other/the others; generic one/one’s/oneself and count-noun substitute one/ones.
- Defining-pronoun group; explicit mapping of additional glossary labels universal and detaching.

## Session 3: interrogative, relative and conjunctive uses
- Who/whom/whose/what/which; subject versus object questions; open versus restricted choices; register.
- Relative clauses: antecedents and pronoun functions, defining/non-defining meanings and commas.
- Who/whom/whose/which/that; object omission, fronted/stranded prepositions, whole-clause which, textbook relative as.
- Conjunctive pronouns in subject/object/predicative clauses; separate analysis of clause function and internal word function.
- Embedded-question statement order; relative that versus conjunction that; what versus an expressed antecedent plus that.

## Session 4: numerals and mathematical English
- Numeral definition; cardinal/ordinal classification; simple/derived/composite formation.
- Syntactic functions: subject, predicative, object, attribute, suitable adverbial uses.
- Spelling/hyphens, irregular ordinals, exact hundred/thousand/million versus approximate plurals; BrE/AmE readings; dates and zero.
- Fractions, mixed numbers, numerator/denominator, decimal fractions, digit-by-digit decimal readings.
- Quantities, agreement, percentages, percentage points versus relative percentage change, measurements and number–unit modifiers.
- Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, operation order and prepositions; sum/difference/product/quotient/remainder.
- Powers, roots, ratios, negative numbers, comparisons, approximation and rounding.
